Our Story

Best Friends First

How We Started

We're best friends first. Sarah brings a warmth and energy into my life and I like to think I keep the two of us grounded. We're easy together — whether it's a good laugh, a long walk with the dogs, or figuring things out side by side, we just work.

We've built a strong foundation, and we can't wait to spend the rest of our lives building on it together.

Our Adventures

We love going on adventures together — usually with our two pups, Cooper and Marley, in tow. Whether it's a hike, a day out on the water, or a trip somewhere we've never been, we're at our best when we're exploring.

When we're not out adventuring, we're happy keeping it simple at home with good food and good company.

The Proposal

And then came the moment everything changed — the question, the "yes," and the beginning of this next chapter. Celebrating that with the people we love most is what this whole weekend is about.

The Venue

The Lodge at
Whitefish Lake

Whitefish, Montana

Nestled on the shores of Whitefish Lake with the Rocky Mountains as a backdrop, The Lodge at Whitefish Lake is the perfect setting for our celebration. Long days, perfect weather, and celebrations right on the water — there's nothing like Whitefish, Montana in the summer.

We're envisioning the tent open to the lake, lush white and cream florals with plenty of greenery, and tables that feel abundant but never fussy. A long, candlelit dinner that gives way to a dance floor that goes late.
