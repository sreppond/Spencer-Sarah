Whitefish, Montana

Lodging

June is peak season in the Flathead, and rooms go early. Book by October and you will have your pick.

  • The Lodge at Whitefish Lake — 1380 Wisconsin Ave, Whitefish, MT 59937 · lodgeatwhitefishlake.com
    Staying where the wedding is. Montana's only AAA Four Diamond resort, with a spa, indoor and outdoor pools, a marina, and the Boat Club Restaurant on site. $$$$
    Room block / rate / booking code: confirming with the venue.
  • Luxury Homes by the Lodge (Viking Creek) — booked through the Lodge · luxuryhomesbythelodge.com
    Full houses on the same property, a short walk from the Main Lodge, with access to all the Lodge's amenities. $$$
    Availability / booking process: confirming.
  • Hidden Moose Lodge — 1735 E Lakeshore Dr, Whitefish, MT 59937 · (406) 862-6516 · hiddenmooselodge.com
    A 15-room B&B about 2 miles from downtown. Full breakfast included, plus a hot tub and a river-rock fireplace lounge. $$$
    Room block / rate: confirming.
  • Hotel Whitefish (currently Grouse Mountain Lodge) — 2 Fairway Dr, Whitefish, MT 59937 · (406) 862-3000
    Mid-renovation now — 72 of 144 rooms already reopened, including a new bridal suite, with a new event pavilion opening fall 2026. The full "Hotel Whitefish" relaunch is targeted for summer 2027, essentially our wedding weekend. $$
    Room block: confirming before we publish rates — the renovation timeline makes this one worth double-checking close to the date.
  • The Firebrand — 650 E 3rd St, Whitefish, MT 59937 · (406) 863-1900 · firebrandhotel.com
    Boutique hotel in the middle of downtown — walk to dinner and the bars after. $$$
    Room block / rate: confirming.
  • Vacation rentals — VRBO / Airbnb, search "Whitefish, MT."
    Best for larger groups or anyone staying longer than the wedding weekend. $$
