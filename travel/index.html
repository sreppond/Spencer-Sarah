Getting Here

Flights to Whitefish

Most people flying in want one answer: can I get there without a connection?

    Glacier Park International (FCA) to Whitefish — 11–12 miles, about 20–25 minutes. There's no wedding-day shuttle arranged yet, so plan on renting a car or arranging your own ride.

    By train, if you want to make an occasion of it

    Amtrak's Empire Builder runs daily between Chicago and Seattle/Portland and stops right in downtown Whitefish — the depot is a few blocks from The Firebrand and about 2 miles from the venue. It's an overnight ride from Seattle or Portland, or a genuinely epic two-night trip from Chicago through Glacier country. Not the fast way to get here, but a memorable one if anyone's got the time. Check amtrak.com for the current Empire Builder schedule closer to June 2027 — long-distance timetables shift.
